REPORT RULE (added after the pilot run showed report templates causing leaks):
Any report, assessment, statistic or summary shown to the player (for example a Master Report or status report) represents only what the player's own government or character currently believes, based on their own sources. Fields such as rebel numbers, enemy strength, unrest or conspiracies must reflect the player's known information. When the player's sources do not know something, write "unknown" or give an estimate clearly labelled as an estimate or rumour. Never fill a report field with hidden information that the player's sources do not have.
